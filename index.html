How I work

I start with search — the one place where people tell you exactly what they want, in their own words. Then I look at what makes someone pick you over the other tab they have open, and write for that. The campaign comes last, not first.

  1. 01

    Search

    Keyword and intent mapping, on-page work, pages built to answer the query properly.

  2. 02

    Story

    Positioning and proof, so the click turns into consideration instead of a bounce.

  3. 03

    Campaign

    Carousels, film and a social rhythm that keep a name familiar while search does its slower work.

  4. 04

    Compound

    The unglamorous part. Pages keep ranking, content keeps earning, the cost of the next customer falls.
