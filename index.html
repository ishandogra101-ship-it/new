for Education

Role

Scope

Sector
EdTech, India

Netoyed sold Google for Education into schools, but most principals had never heard of the category. So the work was closer to teaching than advertising. I built a content engine around one idea, that the schools which move early win: SEO explainers to catch the searches, a weekly social rhythm so the name stayed familiar, launch carousels and short films when we needed momentum.

It reached more than 150 schools, and the Google Reference School programme became the proof point we led every pitch with.

How I work

I start with search, because it is the only place where people tell you exactly what they want, in their own words. Then I look at what makes someone pick you over the other tab they have open, and write for that. The campaign comes last, not first.

  1. 01

    Search

    Keyword and intent mapping, on-page work, and pages built to answer the query properly.

  2. 02

    Story

    Positioning and proof, so the click turns into consideration instead of a bounce.

  3. 03

    Campaign

    Carousels, film and social rhythm that keep a name familiar while search does its slower work.

  4. 04

    Compound

    The unglamorous part. Pages keep ranking, content keeps earning, and the cost of the next customer falls.
