01The story so far

I care about one thing: turning strangers into customers — and customers into advocates.

For the last few years I built brand and pipeline for Netoyed, an EdTech company, as its marketing & growth generalist. I ran the whole engine — SEO that ranks, content that earns attention, launch campaigns, brand films, and organic social — and helped take the product to 150+ schools.

My approach is simple and unglamorous: understand the buyer, build search and content systems that meet them where they already are, then remove every reason not to say yes. Growth that compounds beats growth that spikes.

I'm now working independently — open to roles and projects with teams who want marketing that actually moves the number.

— Alvina Varughese
