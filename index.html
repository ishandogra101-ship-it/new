The fig tree

“I saw my life branching out before me like the green fig tree in the story.”

Sylvia Plath, The Bell Jar

In the book, a young woman sits under a fig tree and starves because she can't choose a fig. I recognised the feeling.

I love research and I love writing. I love strategy and I love building a campaign. I like search, budgets, media plans and meeting new people. Choosing one felt like losing the rest, so when my first job put all of it on my desk, I said yes to every branch.

The tree didn't rot. What I have now is judgement: which work ripens in a week, which in a quarter, and which needs a team around it. I'm ready to go deeper on the branch where strategy shapes search and content, and I'd like to do it somewhere that still values someone who sees the whole tree.
